ABOUT THE COMPANY

Nirmal Bang

Nirmal Bang is an Indian financial-services and capital-markets group with a long presence in the securities industry. The group was founded in 1986 by Late Shri Nirmal M. Bang together with his brothers Mr. Dilip M. Bang and Mr. Kishore M. Bang. Nirmal Bang Securities Private Limited was incorporated on 12 September 1997 and operates as a securities and commodities broking company.

FOUNDERS & LEADERSHIP

The people behind the group

The Nirmal Bang Group was founded by Late Shri Nirmal M. Bang, together with Mr. Dilip M. Bang and Mr. Kishore M. Bang. The group developed its presence in financial markets with a focus on client relationships and investment services.

PRODUCTS & SERVICES

What the company offers

  • Equity and derivatives trading
  • Commodity and forex trading
  • Institutional broking
  • Depository services and Demat accounts
  • Portfolio Management Services (PMS)
  • Mutual-fund and other investment solutions
  • Research and market-analysis services

CLIENTS & POTENTIAL CLIENTS

Who the business serves

The company serves different categories of market participants, including retail investors, High-Net-Worth Individuals (HNIs), institutional investors, mutual funds, financial institutions and corporate clients. Potential clients include individuals and organisations seeking access to financial markets, investment products, trading facilities, research and wealth-management solutions.

WHY THIS COMPANY WAS RELEVANT TO MY INTERNSHIP

My internship gave me practical exposure to the environment in which a financial-services and securities-market organisation works. Through market learning, stock analysis, Demat-account exposure, equity and derivatives concepts and potential-client communication, I was able to connect finance theory with the services offered to real market participants.
