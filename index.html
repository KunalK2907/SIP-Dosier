ABOUT NIRMAL BANG

A relationship
beyond broking.

Founded in 1986, Nirmal Bang is an Indian financial-services and capital-markets group built around helping investors participate in financial markets through broking, investment and research services.

The group was founded by Late Shri Nirmal M. Bang together with his brothers Mr. Dilip M. Bang and Mr. Kishore M. Bang. Today, the organisation operates across the financial-services and securities ecosystem, serving market participants through trading, depository, research and investment-related solutions.
