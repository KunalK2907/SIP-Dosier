# My Internship Canvas — Digital SIP Dossier

This repository presents my Summer Internship experience as a four-page digital portfolio designed for the **Internship Canvas: Transforming Internship Experience into Placement Success** activity.

## Portfolio Structure

### 01 — Company & Industry
- Company profile and internship context
- Industry overview
- Financial markets, securities and investment-product ecosystem

### 02 — Internship & Objectives
- My internship journey
- Learning objectives
- Progress from market awareness to practical exposure

### 03 — Work, Challenges & Learnings
- Demat account exposure
- Stock and market analysis
- Paper and equity trading
- Futures and options
- Potential client calling
- Learning sessions
- Challenges, responses and competencies acquired

### 04 — Conclusion & Career Readiness
- Key outcomes
- Learning reflection
- Placement interview takeaways
- Evidence to support the internship story

## Evidence
The portfolio content is based on the activities recorded in my Internship Progress Reports. Supporting documents such as certificates, approved work samples, photographs or other internship evidence can be added to the repository and linked from the portfolio.

## Purpose
The objective is not only to describe the internship, but to clearly demonstrate:
- What I did
- What I learned
- What challenges I faced
- How I responded
- Which technical and professional competencies I developed
- How the experience prepares me for placement interviews
